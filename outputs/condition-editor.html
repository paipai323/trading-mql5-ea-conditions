My trading conditions

Add conditions, choose a rule for each, and put them in the order you want.
MT5 alerts you once all conditions have passed. This editor does not connect to your account.

Your example: enter a zone → sweep above a level → return to a zone.
Live rules use Bid prices. Completed-candle rules wait for the selected candle to close.

Use your conditions in MT5

  1. Export conditions.csv.
  2. In desktop MT5, select File → Open Data Folder. Copy the CSV into MQL5/Files.
  3. Copy PriceConditionAlerts.ex5 into MQL5/Experts, refresh Navigator, then attach it to your symbol.
  4. Keep MT5 connected and running. After changing the CSV, reattach the EA to load it and start from condition 1.

Each condition requires a separate update, in order. Earlier conditions stay satisfied. OneShot defaults to one final alert; there is no automatic entry. Example prices are illustrative and must be replaced with your own levels.
